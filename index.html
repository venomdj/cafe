Our Story

A quiet rebellion against average coffee.

The Caffeine Hub began with one stubborn idea — that a cup of coffee deserves the same craft as a piece of art. Today, our house roastery sources beans from six countries, our pastry kitchen runs on stone-ground flour, and every chair is placed with intention. Step in, slow down, stay a while.
